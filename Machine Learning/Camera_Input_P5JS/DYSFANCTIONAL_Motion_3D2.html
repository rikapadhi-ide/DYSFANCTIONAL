Model and timeline notes

Simplified from your side-view image: base yaw, arm pitch, then head pitch. Moving the arm also moves and rotates the head. This is not a dimensionally verified CAD model.

Each action starts at 90/90/90°. The original timeline, angle limits, speed and acceleration limits are simulated at 10 ms intervals. Actions 1–3 return to default 4 after 10 seconds, then the preview stops after showing the return.

Mass, current, collisions, servo latency, flex and backlash are not simulated. This does not validate whether the hardware can drive a 250 g head.
