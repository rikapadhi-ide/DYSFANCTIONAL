# Mechanical Design & Fabrication

## Overview

This section documents the physical design, engineering, and fabrication of the anthropomorphic fan.
## Hardware Specifications

- 3-DOF robotic arm: base rotation, arm tilt and head tilt.
- 3 servo motors for joint movement.
- 1 DC motor to spin the fan.
- ESP32-S3 microcontroller.
- 1.28-inch circular display (240 × 240) for eye expressions.
- USB webcam for hand and body tracking.
- Custom 3D-printed base, arm connectors, fan cage and display housing.

## How It Works

The mechanical system provides the physical structure and movement capabilities that bring the fan's personality to life.

Through the integration of motors, mechanical linkages, and fabricated components, the fan is able to express different behaviours through physical movement.
